# App Store Creative Kit

## Positioning

The product is a material learning converter: users bring one difficult PDF, report, paper, or long note, and the app turns it into a visible structure, a guided learning path, active practice, and review.

This kit is for product-page copy, screenshot production, and product-page optimization experiments. It is not a substitute for native iOS packaging or App Store Connect setup.

## Screenshot Storyboard

Target: iPhone 6.7-inch screenshots first. Keep each screenshot focused on one user job.

| Slot | Headline | Screen state | Evidence to show |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 把 PDF 和长文变成练习路径 | Logged-out first screen | One-click sample, four-step promise |
| 2 | 先检索材料结构，不急着总结 | Retrieval stage | Keywords, argument graph, question targets |
| 3 | 系统把结构转成学习路径 | Learning path stage | Progress, guide message, current activity |
| 4 | 用追问和测验检查理解 | Challenge / probe activity | Rubric, choices or answer box, submit action |
| 5 | 学完后自动生成回访 | Review stage | D+1 / D+3 / D+7 review plan |

## Product Page Optimization Variants

| Variant | Hypothesis | First screenshot focus | CTA language |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A: Deep reading | Users searching for paper/PDF reading want structure before summaries. | Retrieval graph and keywords | 先看懂材料结构 |
| B: Exam practice | Students convert notes into active recall faster than rewriting notes. | Challenge and rubric | 把笔记变成练习 |
| C: Research workflow | Researchers need evidence chains and misconception risks. | Argument graph and question targets | 拆论点和证据 |

## App Preview Video Script

Target length: 25-30 seconds.

1. 0-4s: Show a long paper/report entering the app.
2. 4-10s: Show retrieval output: sections, keywords, argument graph, question targets.
3. 10-18s: Show generated learning path and first active exercise.
4. 18-24s: Show feedback, progress advancing, and review plan.
5. 24-30s: End on the promise: "读完不是目标，学会才是。"

## Capture Route

Open `/store-preview` in the web app to view a static screenshot board that mirrors the storyboard. Use it as a composition guide, then replace any static board screenshots with real app-state screenshots before final App Store submission.

## Remaining Store Gaps

- Final icon sizes and App Store Connect icon.
- Real iPhone and iPad screenshots from actual app states.
- Privacy nutrition label source.
- Support URL, marketing URL, review account, and review notes.
- Production App Analytics tied to acquisition source and retention.
